# Known Faces Reference Database

Place high-resolution front-facing portrait photos of individuals here for the facial recognition system.

### Naming Convention:
- The filename (without extension) directly becomes the identified name of the person.
- Examples:
  - `Vijay.jpg` -> Identified as **Vijay**
  - `Ajith.jpg` -> Identified as **Ajith**
  - `Dr_Madhavan.jpg` -> Identified as **Dr Madhavan**
  - `Faculty_CSE.jpg` -> Identified as **Faculty CSE**

### Supported Formats:
- `.jpg` / `.jpeg` (Recommended)
- `.png`
- `.webp`
- `.bmp`

### Best Practices for Highest Accuracy:
1. Ensure good frontal lighting with no heavy shadows across facial landmarks.
2. Neutral facial expression or gentle smile looking directly into the camera lens.
3. No dark sunglasses or extreme angles.
4. Single individual per photo.

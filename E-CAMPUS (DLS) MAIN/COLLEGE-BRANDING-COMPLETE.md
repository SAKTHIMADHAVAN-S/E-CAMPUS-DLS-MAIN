## 📝 Logo Customization

To customize the logo colors, update the SVG fill/stroke attributes:

```xml
<!-- Change primary color (#004080) -->
<circle cx="100" cy="100" r="95" fill="#YOUR_COLOR" />

<!-- Change secondary color (#0073e6) -->
<circle cx="100" cy="100" r="12" fill="#YOUR_COLOR"/>
```
